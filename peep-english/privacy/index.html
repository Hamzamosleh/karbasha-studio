Peep English — Privacy Policy

Karbasha Studio · Last updated: October 3, 2026

Englishالعربية

In short: Peep English has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything it uses stays on your device, and we do not collect, sell or share your data.

Who we are

Peep English is made by Karbasha Studio, an independent developer based in Amman, Jordan. Contact: hamzamosleh90@gmail.com.

What the app uses and why

Data or permissionWhyWhere it goes
Usage access (which app is on screen)To show a word card when you open one of the apps you chose.Read on your device only. It is not stored as a history and never leaves your device.
List of installed appsSo you can pick which apps show word cards.Only the apps you pick are saved, on your device.
Display over other appsTo draw the word card over the app you opened.No data is collected.
Microphone (optional)Only when you tap the microphone button to answer out loud.Your phone's speech recognition service turns the speech into text. Peep English does not record or store audio. That service may be provided by your device maker or Google under their own privacy policies.
NotificationsTo show that word cards are active and to open your saved words.No data is collected.
Your settings and progressLevel, topics, schedule, saved words and learning progress.Stored in the app's private storage on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, location or contacts.
  • We do not send your app usage, settings or progress to any server.
  • We do not show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Your choices

  • You can pause word cards or turn off any permission in Android settings at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app, or using "Clear storage" in Android settings, deletes everything the app saved.

Children

Peep English is a general English-learning tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
