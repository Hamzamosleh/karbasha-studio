Ricoball Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game Ricoball handles. It applies to the Ricoball app only.

What the game stores itself

Your progress and settings are saved on your phone. The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number. Android Auto Backup may copy the save to your own Google account so it survives a reinstall; you can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

The game also keeps a short log of game events (such as stages played) on your phone, and deletes it after 14 days.

Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob and Unity Ads, and uses Unity Analytics to learn how the game is played. These companies may collect and receive the following, to show and measure ads, understand play, keep ads safe and prevent fraud:

  • device and other identifiers, including your advertising ID and App Set ID;
  • approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps, and how you play, such as stages played and results;
  • device and app information and diagnostics.

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Each company handles it under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy and Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Your choices

The game asks for your choice the first time it matters. If you choose "No thanks", or switch off SHARE PLAY DATA in Settings, ads still appear, but the game asks these companies not to personalise them and sends no play data to Unity Analytics. In some regions Google also shows its own consent form. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Keeping and deleting data

Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. We don't hold any copy of it. For data held by these companies, use the links above, or write to us and we'll help.

Children

Ricoball is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. For questions or deletion requests, write to Karbasha Studio at karbashastudio26@gmail.com.
